Communication Standard

Corporate communication should arrive with enough context to be routed correctly.

Concise identification of the organization, subject, and purpose of an inquiry helps RPE distinguish legitimate enterprise correspondence from product support, unsolicited promotion, and unrelated requests.

01 · Identify

State who is contacting RPE.

Include your name, organization where applicable, and an appropriate return contact method.

02 · Context

State the subject clearly.

Identify the relevant system, market, business matter, media request, or institutional subject.

03 · Purpose

State the requested action.

Explain what you are requesting from RPE so the correspondence can be evaluated and routed appropriately.
