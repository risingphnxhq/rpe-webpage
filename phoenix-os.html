Phoenix OS

Proprietary enterprise foundation.

Phoenix OS is Rising Phoenix Enterprises’ proprietary technology environment supporting the company’s growing portfolio of specialized systems across multiple industries. It reflects RPE’s long-term commitment to developing technology that reduces complexity, improves continuity, and strengthens the ability of people and organizations to operate effectively.

Role

Foundation.

Phoenix OS supports RPE systems without being presented as a public product environment.

Purpose

Continuity.

It allows RPE to maintain consistent institutional direction across systems serving different markets.

Scope

Portfolio support.

It supports current systems and future industry expansion without exposing internal construction methods.

System Lineage

Different industries. One enterprise foundation.

STATS-CORE, Phoenix Back Office, Phoenix Business System, and future RPE systems each serve different markets. Phoenix OS is the proprietary foundation beneath that portfolio.
