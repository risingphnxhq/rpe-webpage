The Institution

A multi-industry enterprise by design.

RPE is not organized around one application, one market, or one technology cycle. The company identifies persistent operating problems and develops systems intended to improve continuity, access, clarity, coordination, and performance within the environments they serve.

Purpose

Increase capacity.

Reduce recurring complexity and strengthen the ability of people and organizations to function effectively.

Position

Build across industries.

Develop distinct systems for distinct operating environments rather than confining the enterprise to one sector.

Direction

Think long-range.

Build an enduring portfolio capable of serving expanding domestic and international markets.
